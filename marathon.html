Every training day

  1. Walk briskly for 5 minutes to warm up.
  2. Do the main session.
  3. Walk slowly for 5 minutes to cool down, then stretch calves, thighs and hips for 3 to 5 minutes.

Jog pace: slow enough that you can talk in short sentences. If you can't, slow down or walk.

Rest days: either full rest, or an easy 20 to 30 minute walk. Some rest days also suggest light strength work.

Full plan

Race day tips

  • Start slower than feels natural. Most people burn out in the first kilometer.
  • Walk breaks are fine, and you've trained for them.
  • Wear only shoes and clothes you've already trained in.
  • Eat a light breakfast 2 to 3 hours before, and sip water.

Adjustments

  • If you miss a session, don't double up. Just repeat the missed one on your next training day.
  • If a session feels too hard, repeat the previous one before moving on.
  • If you feel sharp pain (not just tired muscles), skip the next session. At your weight, a gradual build-up protects your knees and shins.
  • If you have any heart, joint or blood pressure concerns, check with a doctor before starting.
