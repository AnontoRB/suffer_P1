Calendar

Green = all done · red outline = unfinished on a past day · coloured border = has tasks · dashed = today. Tap a date to see and add its tasks. Switch the box to Category to make a new category, or manage all categories further down.

Categories

Make a category once, like Study or Home, and it stays here until you delete it. Pick it when you add a task, or add tasks to it directly below. Any task can also hold subtasks.
