Fitness plan: ready for 7.5 km

From Nov 8 to race day on Jan 6. Bodyweight strength, run/walk training that builds to 7.5 km, plus water and sleep tracking. Built from your start: 8 push-ups, 30 sit-ups, 30 squats, no pull-ups, a 30-second sprint, and 40–60 minutes of easy walking.

Week by week

Weekly pattern: Run 1, Strength A, Run 2, Strength B, rest walk, Long run, Strength C. Weeks 4 and 8 are lighter so your body can adapt. If a week feels too hard, repeat the previous week.

Progressive overload guide

  • Change one thing at a time: reps, sets, slower tempo or shorter rest.
  • Double progression: hold a rep target until every set feels easy, then add 1–2 reps.
  • Leave 2 reps in the tank in early weeks. Form first.
  • Slow the lowering to 3 seconds on push-ups and squats to make the same reps harder.
  • Push-up ladder: knee push-ups, full, slow full, feet raised on a step, pike push-ups.
  • Squat ladder: squats, slow squats, reverse lunges, split squats, single-leg sit-to-stand.
  • Running: never raise weekly running time by more than about 10–15%. Easy pace means you can say a short sentence.
  • Pull-ups: can't be trained with zero equipment. Superman holds build your back for now; with a bar, start with hangs and slow lowering.
  • Sharp or joint pain, or shin pain that worsens means rest and drop back a week.

7.5 km race tips

Run the first kilometre slower than feels natural. Walking breaks are fine, and many finishers use them. Drink at the stations, eat a light breakfast 2–3 hours before, and lay out your kit the night before. This plan peaks with a 50-minute jog on Dec 25, so 7.5 km on race day is realistic. If anything hurts, back off rather than push through, and check with a doctor before raising mileage.

Water and sleep

  • Water: 3 L a day (more on hot or long-run days). A glass on waking and one before each meal.
  • Sleep: 8 hours, for example 22:30 to 06:30, at the same times every day. Gains happen while you sleep.
General fitness guidance, not medical advice. Stop for chest pain, dizziness or sharp pain.
